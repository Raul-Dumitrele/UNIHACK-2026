# Provocări alese (Challenge Bank, Community Hero)

## CH-08 Public Utilities - "When does my hot water come back?"
Problem: many apartments get heating and hot water from Colterm. In Oct 2021 its gas supply was cut, leaving about 50,000 households, plus hospitals and schools, without heat or hot water. Notices about planned cuts and breakdowns of water, heat and power are spread across utilities' websites, social media and notes taped to building doors. Residents rarely know whether their own building is affected or when service will return.
Challenge: How might we tell every household, building by building, when water, heat or power will go off and when it will come back?

## CH-09 Transit Detours - "Months of detours"
Problem: roadworks from August to end of 2026 suspended tram line 5 and detoured lines 1 and 2. Lines 6a and 6b were already suspended; temporary bus line 5C was added. Changes come out as press releases, so it's easy to wait at a stop no tram serves any more.
Challenge: How might we make sure no one waits at a stop that no tram will serve during months of works?
